Photo Doctor 0.5.3.18

Surface-defect detection and verification overhaul.

1. Context-aware surface detector v4
- multi-scale bright/dark morphology remains the candidate generator;
- candidates are now evaluated using local-neighborhood contrast, line geometry, true oriented aspect, estimated stroke width, side similarity, texture risk and chroma risk;
- repeated near-parallel lines are penalized/suppressed as likely fabric/text/blinds structure;
- face and eye regions raise the evidence bar without hiding possible real scratches;
- poor-context candidates are removed before AI inference.

2. Surface AI safety
- embedded Surface Refiner v1 is explicitly treated as a synthetic/procedural baseline;
- a confident v1 prediction cannot promote a poor classical candidate by itself;
- final verification fuses AI evidence with deterministic context evidence;
- dark natural lines are handled especially conservatively;
- raw AI verdict and final verification verdict are retained separately for diagnostics.

3. Real-photo study
- user-supplied damaged/restored pairs were used as weak real-pair supervision for calibration research;
- original photos are not bundled with Photo Doctor;
- a candidate learned calibrator was rejected because cross-source validation was not strong enough;
- real-pair findings were used to calibrate detector/fusion behavior, not to masquerade weak labels as Expert Base.

4. Diagnostics / UI truthfulness
- AI resources text states that Surface Refiner v1 is a synthetic baseline and its result is context-checked;
- surface refinement diagnostics report raw AI counts separately from final verified counts.
