Photo Doctor 0.5.10 — MAXIMUM
Date: 2026-09-24

Main changes
============

1. Fourth precision mode renamed to "Максимальный"
- Public GUI/CLI profile key: `maximum`, label "Максимальный".
- Legacy `ideal` / `идеально` remain accepted only as compatibility aliases for old settings/scripts.
- Fast / Normal / Precise are unchanged.

2. Maximum now uses a materially denser spatial decomposition
- The first Maximum draft (~21.9k local windows on 4K) was intentionally deepened again.
- Final 4K local-map budget is ~42,928 overlapping measurements across local sharpness, tone and contrast.
- On the archival reference 479x781 image Maximum uses ~9,033 map cells (3375 + 2829 + 2829), versus ~1,086 in Precise and ~2,333 in the old 0.5.9 Ideal mode.
- Scalar quality scores remain calibrated on stable summary windows; the extra cells improve localisation rather than silently changing quality thresholds.

3. Maximum Surface search is substantially fuller
- Surface V9 remains additive: proven Precise geometry is retained and deeper Maximum candidates are merged on top.
- Maximum also runs two independent local-normalisation proposal passes (different CLAHE grids) to expose faint scratches/cracks under broad illumination gradients and short broken damage fragments.
- Visible/deep Surface budgets are 256/320 in Maximum.
- Native Surface AI budget is raised so the full Maximum candidate pool can be reviewed instead of leaving late candidates as "unprocessed".
- On the archival reference: 232 Surface candidates, 232 AI-refined, 0 unprocessed.

4. Surface repair remains strictly manual-only
- Search can be deliberately sensitive because it is not allowed to repair by itself.
- Validator keeps `accepted=False` and `auto_eligible=False` for Surface.
- No repair trial/inpaint is run during ordinary analysis; it is deferred until the user explicitly selects Defects rows.
- Surface history/convergence diagnostics are preserved without modifying the photo.

5. Result tab remains read-only
- The first/result tab contains only the correction summary; no user-selectable correction checkboxes are shown there.
- Correction choices live in Plan and specialised tabs; Surface treatment is chosen on Defects.

Runtime reference
=================
Same archival regression image, separate processes on the current environment:
- Precise: ~5.62 s, 61 Surface candidates.
- 0.5.9 old Ideal: ~10.75 s, 76 Surface candidates.
- 0.5.10 Maximum: ~20.57 s, 232 Surface candidates, 0 unprocessed.
- Maximum is therefore ~3.66x Precise and ~1.91x the old fourth mode on this image.

The intentionally rejected draft exceeded 2 minutes on the same small portrait because face detection was brute-forced across too many near-duplicate rotations. That was removed: the extra Maximum budget is spent primarily on spatial localisation and Surface defect search, not redundant face scans.
